Privacy Policy for InMailer

Last Updated: August 23, 2025

Quick Summary: We only collect the information necessary to provide our email service. We use Google OAuth for secure login and Gmail API to send emails on your behalf. We never sell your data or access your existing Gmail messages.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name and email address when you sign up
  • Email Templates: Content you create for email campaigns
  • Contact Lists: Email addresses and data you upload
  • Email Content: Messages you compose and send

Information from Google

When you sign in with Google, we access:

  • Your Google profile information (name, email address)
  • Gmail sending permissions (to send emails on your behalf)
Important: We do NOT access your existing Gmail messages, contacts, or any other Google data beyond what's necessary for our service.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide Service: Enable email sending, template management, and tracking
  • Account Management: Create and maintain your account
  • Service Improvement: Analyze usage to improve features
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraud and abuse

Google API Services Usage

InMailer uses Google API Services to:

  • Authenticate Users: Verify your identity through Google OAuth
  • Send Emails: Send emails through Gmail API on your behalf
  • Access Profile: Retrieve your basic profile information

We comply with Google API Services User Data Policy.

Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information.

We may share information only with:

  • Service Providers: Gmail API, Neon.tech (database), Render.com/Vercel.com (hosting)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

Data Security

  • Encryption: Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
  • Access Controls: Limited access to authorized personnel only
  • Secure Authentication: OAuth 2.0 with Google
  • Regular Monitoring: Continuous security monitoring

Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Update: Modify your account information
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export: Download your templates and contact lists

Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Email Logs: Stored for 90 days for tracking
  • Templates: Retained until you delete them

Contact Information

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights:

Email: privacy@inmailer.app

Website: https://inmailer.vercel.app

Compliance

This Privacy Policy complies with:

  • GDPR (European General Data Protection Regulation)
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  • Google API Services User Data Policy
  • CAN-SPAM Act

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of material changes via email and post updates on our website.

Effective Date: August 23, 2025

By using InMailer, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
