# Privacy Policy for InMailer

**Last Updated:** August 23, 2025

## Introduction

InMailer ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our email outreach application.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Information:** Name, email address when you sign up
- **Email Templates:** Content you create for email campaigns
- **Contact Lists:** Email addresses and associated data you upload
- **Email Content:** Messages you compose and send through our service

### Information We Collect Automatically
- **Usage Data:** How you interact with our service, features used, time spent
- **Device Information:** Browser type, operating system, IP address
- **Log Data:** Server logs, error reports, performance metrics

### Information from Third-Party Services
- **Google Account Data:** When you sign in with Google, we access:
  - Your Google profile information (name, email address)
  - Gmail sending permissions (to send emails on your behalf)
  - We do NOT access your existing Gmail messages or contacts

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- **Provide Service:** Enable email sending, template management, and campaign tracking
- **Account Management:** Create and maintain your account, authenticate users
- **Service Improvement:** Analyze usage patterns to improve our features
- **Communication:** Send service-related notifications and updates
- **Security:** Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents

## Google API Services Usage

InMailer uses Google API Services to:
- **Authenticate Users:** Verify your identity through Google OAuth
- **Send Emails:** Send emails through Gmail API on your behalf
- **Access Profile:** Retrieve your basic profile information (name, email)

### Google API Data Usage Restrictions
- We only request the minimum permissions necessary for our service
- We do not store your Google account credentials
- We do not access your existing Gmail messages or contacts
- We comply with Google API Services User Data Policy

### Service Providers

### Legal Requirements
- When required by law, court order, or government request
- To protect our rights, property, or safety
- To prevent fraud or security threats

### Business Transfers
- In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with user notification)

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- **Encryption:** Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
- **Access Controls:** Limited access to authorized personnel only
- **Secure Authentication:** OAuth 2.0 with Google for secure login
- **Regular Monitoring:** Continuous monitoring for security threats
- **Data Backup:** Regular backups with encryption

## Data Retention

- **Account Data:** Retained while your account is active
- **Email Logs:** Stored for 90 days for delivery tracking and troubleshooting
- **Templates:** Retained until you delete them
- **Usage Analytics:** Aggregated data retained for service improvement
